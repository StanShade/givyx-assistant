# Call plan — 2026-09-09 · dealerships (komisy)

**Why this segment:** 12 workshop touches → 10 conversations → **0 closes**. The killer objection was
never price — it was **need**: "mam dużo klientów" (G-Performance yesterday, Cyganik, + 2 more).
Workshops are **capacity-constrained**; they turn work away. A dealer is the opposite:
**inventory-constrained** — unsold stock is capital depreciating on the forecourt.

**The frame — do NOT say "your website is weak".** That earned attention and zero sales for 12 calls.
Say instead: **"you already pay Otomoto to show these cars — and your own site shows none of them."**
That is a leak, not a lack. Verified anchor: **21–30 cars = 4 159,99 zł net / 30 days** (Otomoto's own
published pricing). Our top tier is 750 zł.

**The thing a znajomy cannot do:** Otomoto's **API export is in EVERY package incl. Standard**, and
these dealers' accounts already carry `API_ACCESS`. Their stock can load onto their own site
automatically. A friend with WordPress cannot build that.
